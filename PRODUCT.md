# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed by the owner):

- **Evaluators** (recruiters, hiring engineers, course reviewers) who open the published War Room with about two minutes and may never run the code. They need to grasp the thesis, "the model proposes; code and humans decide", from the replay alone.
- **SRE / on-call engineers** who read it as an operational tool and scrutinize it: what each agent did, which tier an action has, why the gate stopped, what the numbers are.

## Product Purpose

incident-copilot is a multi-agent incident copilot (LangGraph + TypeScript). A supervisor coordinates telemetry, runbook, planner and auditor agents; risky actions pass through an autonomy matrix with a human approval gate; forbidden actions never run; MTTR and savings are computed by code, not by the LLM. The War Room is the static, publishable replay of runs recorded by the backend itself: agent conversation, approval gate (approve and reject branches), numbers, and post-mortem. Success: a visitor understands who decided what, and trusts that nothing was invented.

## Positioning

The model never owns the decision. Tier comes from a deterministic catalog, approval comes from a human, numbers come from pure functions, and the audit is a hash chain. The War Room makes that division of authority visible.

## Operating Context

- Static page on GitHub Pages, fetching only `./demo/` recordings validated with Zod (`DemoRecordingSchema`).
- Two recorded scenarios: `deploy-5xx-rollback` and `cost-anomaly`, each with approve and reject branches.
- Replay controls: step, play/pause, choose a branch at the gate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AC-39 (binding): the label "Replay of a recorded run with a scripted fake provider" on every screen; fetch only `./demo/`; explicit error for invalid recordings; focus trap in the approval dialog (ESC closes and returns focus); tier conveyed by text and icon, not color alone; text/background pairs at least 4.5:1, checked by `tokens-contrast.test.ts`; axe tests pass.
- Design tokens live only in `web/src/styles/tokens.css` (`npm run check:tokens`), light and dark themes.
- React 19 + Vite 8, nested `web/` package. New dependencies need justification and an exact version.
- The War Room never computes metrics; it shows what the recording carries.
- All human-facing text is English, en-US number formats.
- Below 600px: single column, no horizontal scroll, fully keyboard operable.

## Evidence on Hand

Real backend recordings generated by `npm run demo:record` into `web/public/demo/`. No testimonials, customers or production claims exist; none may be invented. Every run shown is a replay with a fake provider and must say so.

## Product Principles

1. Show who decided: model, code, or human, on every consequential event.
2. Never imply live execution; the replay is honest about being a replay.
3. Numbers are evidence, shown with their provenance, never decoration.
4. Readable under pressure: scannable for an on-call engineer, legible in two minutes for an evaluator.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) in both themes, full keyboard operation, focus management at the gate and at the end of the replay, screen-reader announcements that do not flood, respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
